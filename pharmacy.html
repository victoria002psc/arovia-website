Pharmacy Coordination & Dispensation

Pharmacy Services & Medicine Information

Call Arovia's pharmacy desk to enquire about medicines and healthcare supplies for ongoing treatment, recovery after a procedure, and day-to-day care. Availability, prescription requirements, and the applicable price are confirmed with you on the call.

* Direct phone consultation only. Prescription medicines are supplied only after the required prescription is checked, in line with statutory drug regulations.

Responsible Pharmaceutical Dispensation & Prescription Standards

In accordance with the Drugs and Cosmetics Act and national pharmaceutical regulations, prescription medicines (including Schedule H, H1, and X therapeutic agents) require a valid, legible prescription issued by a registered medical practitioner. Arovia does not dispense prescription medicines without that check, and this website does not offer online ordering or checkout. Call the pharmacy desk to enquire. Whether an item can be supplied, and the price that applies, is confirmed during that conversation.

Responsible Dispensing and Product Checks

Before a medicine or healthcare supply goes ahead, the pharmacy desk works through the details of that enquiry. These are dispensing checks. They are not a laboratory test, a batch-tracking system, or a guarantee that packaging alone proves a product is authentic.

Prescription and dispensing requirements

Where a prescription is required, the desk checks that it is available and legible, and that the details needed to dispense are clear. Missing or unclear information is clarified before the enquiry proceeds.

Name, strength, formulation, and quantity

Where a prescription applies, the request is matched to the medicine name, strength, formulation, and quantity written on it. A mismatch is not resolved by changing the prescribed treatment on your behalf.

Packaging and expiry

Relevant packaging and expiry information are reviewed before a product is supplied. This check supports safe dispensing. It does not, by itself, certify authenticity.

If something does not look right

If a product appears damaged, incorrect, or questionable, do not use it. Call the pharmacy desk with the product details so the concern can be clarified. Unclear clinical instructions should go back to the treating healthcare professional.

Medication Categories

Understanding Healthcare & Medicine Categories

These four areas describe the kinds of enquiries the pharmacy desk handles. Individual medicines are not listed on this page.

Chronic Disease Maintenance

Ongoing medicines for long-term conditions, including treatments a doctor has prescribed for the heart, blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid, or breathing. If a repeat supply is running low, call before it finishes so availability can be checked. This page does not advise you to start, stop, or change a treatment.

Post-Surgical & Acute Therapies

Enquiries for medicines and supplies prescribed after discharge or a procedure, including the course written by the treating doctor. If a discharge note or dose is unclear, ask that healthcare professional before requesting a different product.

Clinical & Nursing Consumables

Enquiries for wound-care, dressing, and nursing supplies used during recovery at home. When you call, share the specification if you know it — for example the dressing type, size, or the item named in the care instructions.

Supportive Wellness & First Aid

General healthcare and first-aid items, such as oral rehydration salts, antiseptic solutions, and simple bedside supplies. Not every item is held at all times, and this category is not a prompt to self-medicate. For a new or worsening symptom, speak to a healthcare professional.

Inquiry Process

What to Prepare When Calling the Pharmacy Desk

Have these details ready so the desk can check the requirement. The desk does not set or change a prescribed dose.

1

Doctor's Written Prescription

Have your current doctor's prescription clearly accessible. Ensure it includes the doctor's name, medical registration number, patient name, date of consultation, and precise medication instructions.

2

Specific Strength & Dosage Formulation

Confirm whether the prescription specifies tablets, capsules, oral syrups, topical ointments, or injectables, along with exact strengths (e.g. 250 mg, 500 mg, 10 mg/ml, or sustained-release formulations).

3

Required Duration & Pack Quantity

Know the prescribed duration of treatment—whether you are filling a 5-day acute post-surgical antibiotic regimen or an ongoing 30-day chronic maintenance supply for cardiac or diabetic care.

4

Patient Sensitivities or Known Allergies

Inform the pharmacy coordinator of any known patient drug allergies (such as penicillin, sulfa drugs, or NSAIDs) so appropriate notes and precautions can be referenced before dispensing.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Pharmacy Services

Short answers about enquiries, prescriptions, pricing, and what the pharmacy desk confirms by phone.

Do I need a prescription to enquire about a medicine?

Prescription medicines, including Schedule H, H1, and X products, need a valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner. You may still call to ask what is required. General first-aid and many over-the-counter items do not need a prescription.

How do I confirm whether a medicine is available?

Call the pharmacy desk with the prescription or product details. Availability is confirmed on that call. This page does not show live stock.

What happens if my requested medicine is unavailable?

The desk will tell you it cannot be supplied at that time. Arovia does not automatically source a replacement. Ask the treating prescriber before changing a prescribed medicine, dose, or formulation.

How is the applicable price confirmed?

The pharmacy desk states the price on the call, before you proceed. Prices are not listed here. Packaged medicines in India also carry a manufacturer maximum retail price under National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority rules.

Can a family member enquire on behalf of a patient?

Yes, a family member or caregiver may call to ask about a requirement. Any extra step for collecting a prescription medicine is explained by the desk on that call. It is not set out on this page.

How do I confirm collection or delivery arrangements?

Ask the pharmacy desk during the enquiry. Collection is from the Guntur premises. Whether delivery is possible for your location is confirmed then, along with any charge. Do not assume a delivery until it is agreed.

What should I do if a supplied product appears damaged or incorrect?

Do not use it. Call the pharmacy desk, name the product, and describe what looks wrong. The desk will explain the next step. A separate returns policy is not published on this page.

What information should I keep ready before calling?

The prescription when one is required, or the product name, plus strength, formulation, and quantity. Mention any drug allergy you already know about.

Can the pharmacy change my dose or substitute a medicine?

No. Arovia does not change a prescribed dose, strength, or medicine on its own. Any change must come from the treating healthcare professional.

Why is there no medicine list or online checkout?

Individual medicines are not displayed, and there is no cart or checkout. The pharmacy desk confirms the requirement, availability, and price directly, so a supply is not placed through the website.
