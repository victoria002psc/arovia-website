Helpful Guidelines

What to Have Ready When You Call

To help our team assist you quickly and accurately, have the relevant details ready when you telephone:

Home Care Coordination

Clarify whether you need non-clinical Caregiver assistance (daily living support, bathing, mobility) or Registered Nursing (clinical observations, physician-prescribed injections, sterile wound care), patient condition, and location in Guntur.

Call Home Care Desk

Ambulance Transportation

State the exact pickup address (including apartment, floor, and elevator status), destination hospital or residence, and patient posture (stretcher or wheelchair) so our desk can verify vehicle availability and confirm applicable charges.

Call Ambulance Desk

Medical Equipment (Export Supply)

Mention the specific product name or catalog model code (e.g., AV-HET ICU Bed, AV-EI3A Electric Bed, AV-PM7 Multi-Para Monitor) to request formal proforma invoices and discuss international export shipping terms.

Call Equipment Desk

Pharmacy & Prescription Inquiries

Have your registered doctor's prescription ready for verification, and specify the medication brand/generic names, dosage strengths (e.g. 250 mg or 500 mg), and required pack quantities.

Call Pharmacy Desk

Diagnostic Lab Tests

Specify the tests or health check packages required, confirm fasting status (e.g. 8-12 hour overnight fasting for glucose or lipid panels), and discuss testing arrangements and pricing with our team.

Call Lab Desk

Why We Operate Exclusively by Direct Telephone

Healthcare needs are personal, urgent, and unique. In critical situations—such as requesting medical transportation, arranging a registered nurse for clinical procedures, or confirming hospital equipment—online forms create unnecessary delays and automated checkout systems cannot assess medical needs.

By placing our official phone helpline at the center of our service, we ensure every caller speaks directly to a knowledgeable healthcare coordinator without forms, spam filters, or account sign-ups.

Communication Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions About Contacting Arovia

Answers regarding our telephone-first model, response arrangements, and visiting our office.

Why does Arovia prioritize telephone calls instead of online contact forms?

Healthcare needs involve nuanced clinical details—such as patient mobility status, specific medical dosages, and physical access conditions—that automated forms cannot evaluate safely. Speaking directly with our healthcare coordinator ensures immediate mutual understanding without waiting for email replies.

Does calling Arovia guarantee immediate service availability?

Calling connects you directly with our coordinator to verify real-time availability in your area. Service provision depends on nurse schedules, vehicle routes, and current clinical capacity, which is why immediate verbal confirmation over the telephone is required before finalizing arrangements.

Can I visit the Arovia office in Guntur in person?

Yes, our registered office is located at Shop No.26, Ground Floor, Annapurna Complex, Kakani Road, Guntur. We recommend telephoning ahead to ensure the relevant department coordinator is present to assist you.

What should I do if someone needs immediate emergency medical intervention?

For sudden, life-threatening medical emergencies, always call statutory emergency services (such as 108 or 112 in India) immediately for priority dispatch. Arovia provides scheduled, assisted, and non-emergency healthcare coordination.
