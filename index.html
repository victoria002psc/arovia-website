Understanding Home Care Options

Two Distinct Types of Home Care at Arovia

To help families choose the right level of support, Arovia clearly separates everyday non-clinical caregiving from professional clinical nursing care.

Personal Daily Assistance

Caregivers (Attendants)

Caregivers provide non-medical, personal day-to-day assistance for older adults, patients with limited mobility, or individuals recovering who need practical help with ordinary daily living activities.

Assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, and personal hygiene
Meal feeding assistance and fluid intake support
Help with walking, bed-to-chair transfers, and fall prevention
Attentive companionship, emotional reassurance, and routine support
Learn More About Caregiver Support
Clinical Healthcare

Registered Nurses

Registered nurses deliver specialized clinical healthcare procedures at home for post-surgical patients, individuals requiring medical procedures, and patients under ongoing medical supervision.

Physician-prescribed injections and intravenous (IV) infusion therapy
Sterile post-operative wound dressings and bed-sore prevention
Catheter care, Ryle's tube feeding assistance, and routine lines care
Vital sign monitoring, clinical observations, and updates to the doctor

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers to common questions about Arovia's healthcare coordination and services.

What is Arovia and what services do you provide?

Arovia is a healthcare service coordination provider offering access to five distinct categories: home care (caregivers and registered nurses), ambulance patient transportation, hospital-grade medical equipment for international shipping, pharmacy coordination, and diagnostic laboratory testing.

How do I enquire about or arrange a service?

We operate on a direct phone-first model. Review the information on our service pages to understand what is offered, then call our coordination desk directly. You will speak directly with a team member who can discuss your specific situation and guide you accordingly.

Do I need an online account or mobile app to use Arovia?

No. Arovia is completely accessible without downloading apps, creating accounts, or entering credit card details online. We believe healthcare communication should be personal, direct, and unhindered by digital barriers.

How do I know whether my patient needs a caregiver or a nurse?

If the patient primarily needs help with everyday physical activities—such as bathing, dressing, meal assistance, mobility, and companionship—a caregiver is appropriate. If the patient requires medical procedures such as injections, IV therapy, sterile wound dressing, or clinical vital sign monitoring, a registered nurse is required under physician guidance. You can also call us to discuss your patient's routine.

How do medical equipment orders and export enquiries work?

We coordinate hospital-grade furniture, ICU beds, and diagnostic monitoring devices for healthcare facilities and international export enquiry. Browse our catalog specifications, then call our medical desk to discuss bulk quantities, obtain proforma quotations, and verify shipping schedules.

How quickly can an ambulance or patient transit be arranged?

For patient transit needs, whether scheduled clinical transfers or discharge transports, call our dispatch line directly. Our coordinators immediately verify destination distance, route requirements, and necessary clinical assistance (such as oxygen support or recumbent stretcher transit) without automated delay.
