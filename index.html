Understanding Home Care Options

Two Types of Home Care at Arovia

To help families choose the right kind of assistance, Arovia provides two distinct categories of home care: general caregivers for day-to-day physical assistance, and registered nurses for medical and clinical procedures.

Personal Assistance

Caregivers (Attendants)

Caregivers provide non-medical, personal day-to-day assistance for elderly individuals, patients with limited mobility, or those recovering who need help with routine daily activities.

Help with bathing, dressing, and personal hygiene
Meal feeding assistance and fluid intake support
Assisting with walking, transfers, and bed repositioning
Friendly companionship and reassurance at home
Learn More About Caregiver Support
Clinical Care

Registered Nurses

Registered nurses deliver specialized clinical care directly at home for post-surgical patients, ICU step-down cases, and patients requiring professional medical monitoring.

Intravenous (IV) medication and infusion therapy
Post-operative wound dressing and bed-sore prevention
Catheter, Ryle's tube, and tracheostomy clinical care
Continuous vital sign monitoring and doctor reporting
Learn More About Clinical Nursing
