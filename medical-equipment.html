Clinical Guidance on Selecting Equipment

Selecting appropriate medical equipment requires careful clinical alignment with patient acuity and facility protocols. Before procuring critical care items such as ICU beds, multi-parameter monitors, or infusion pumps, always consult a qualified healthcare professional, attending physician, or biomedical engineer to confirm clinical suitability and power/installation requirements.

International Export Only Notice

Arovia supplies medical equipment exclusively for international export and institutional shipments. Deliveries are packaged to export standards. Because port clearances, freight rates, and import regulations vary by destination country, serviceable routes and exact delivery timelines are confirmed individually upon telephone inquiry.

Procurement & Technical Insights

Frequently Asked Questions About Medical Equipment

Key questions regarding product specifications, export shipping, ordering procedures, and clinical selection.

What product categories are included in the Arovia equipment catalog?

The 28-item catalog includes five core categories: Hospital Beds & Stretchers (such as motorized ICU beds and emergency trolleys), OT & Ward Furniture (crash carts, bedside lockers, instrument tables, and examination stools), Patient Mobility (folding and fixed transit wheelchairs), Diagnostic & Monitoring Devices (multi-parameter patient monitors, 12-channel ECG machines, Holter systems, and digital thermometers), and Critical Care & Treatment (biphasic defibrillators and micro-infusion pumps).

Why are prices not listed on the website?

Because our equipment is supplied through institutional export, pricing varies based on order volume, customized accessories, international freight method (ocean vs. air cargo), and destination import duties. We provide tailored, transparent telephone quotes and formal Proforma Invoices for all inquiries.

Is this equipment intended for local retail or international export?

The medical equipment catalog is structured strictly for international export and institutional supply. Buyers outside India can coordinate directly with our desk to establish serviceable shipping destinations, documentation, and wire transfer terms.

How should buyers determine the appropriate equipment specifications?

Equipment selection must be guided by qualified healthcare professionals or hospital biomedical departments. Important factors include clinical intended use, room dimensions, patient weight limits, electrical voltage compatibility (220V vs. other standards), and cleaning/sanitization protocols.

What are the standard international payment and order terms?

Standard terms require a 30% advance deposit upon acceptance of the formal Proforma Invoice, with the remaining 70% balance payable prior to shipment against agreed export shipping documents. For qualified institutional accounts, Irrevocable Letters of Credit (LC) at sight may be accommodated. Payments are settled in USD via bank wire transfer.

How are warranties and manufacturer support handled?

Products carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty against manufacturing defects, or the manufacturer's actual warranty period where expressly specified. Routine wear and tear, consumables, physical damage, and unauthorized alterations are excluded under standard warranty terms.
