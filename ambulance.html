Service Scope

What the Ambulance Service is For

Our ambulance transportation service is intended for patients who require assisted, recumbent, or medically supported transport that cannot be comfortably managed in a standard passenger vehicle:

Hospital Admissions & Consultations

Transportation for planned medical admissions, doctor consultations, chemotherapy visits, or dialysis sessions where patient comfort and stretcher transit are required.

Post-Treatment Hospital Discharges

Safe return travel from the hospital to the patient's residence after surgery, inpatient therapy, or specialized treatment, accompanied by supportive handling.

Inter-Facility Transfers

Assisted transfers between different clinics, diagnostic centers, nursing homes, and regional healthcare institutions according to patient requirements.

Helpful Guide

Details to Have Ready When You Call

To help our coordinator give you accurate information promptly, it is helpful to have the following details ready when you call. This is provided as helpful guidance, not a mandatory checklist:

1

Pickup Location Details

The exact address, including apartment name, floor number, elevator availability, and a well-known nearby landmark to help the driver navigate smoothly.

2

Destination Hospital or Residence

The destination facility name, specific wing/ward (for hospital arrivals), or the residential address where the patient is to be transferred.

3

Patient's Physical Condition & Transportation Needs

Whether the patient can sit upright or needs to remain lying down on a stretcher, whether assistance is required for carrying or stairs, and whether ongoing oxygen or an accompanying family member will travel with them.

4

Preferred Timing

Whether you need assistance as soon as a vehicle can be scheduled, or if you are coordinating in advance for a planned hospital discharge or scheduled specialist appointment.

Transparent Pricing

Confirming Availability and Applicable Charges

Ambulance availability and transportation fees vary depending on the travel distance, pickup route, and the specific patient handling requirements involved.

How Pricing & Availability Are Handled:

When you call our coordinator, they will review your pickup location and destination to check if a vehicle is currently free or can be scheduled for your desired time.

Any applicable standard charges—including base transportation fees and distance-related costs—will be clearly stated over the phone before you confirm the arrangement. There are no hidden digital fees or automated booking charges.
