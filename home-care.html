Non-Clinical Personal Support

Caregivers — Help With Everyday Activities

Everyday activities can become difficult when someone is older, recovering, or needs extra support. A caregiver helps with personal routines and daily tasks, allowing the person to receive assistance in their own home.

Our caregivers provide respectful, patient assistance tailored to each individual's comfort and dignity, helping families maintain steady daily schedules.

A caregiver providing daily mobility assistance and friendly company at home

Services Provided by Caregivers

The following everyday non-clinical activities are provided by our trained home caregivers:

Personal grooming and dressing

Caregivers can assist with getting dressed, changing clothes, grooming, and other personal routines according to the person's needs.

Bathing and personal hygiene

Provide assistance with bathing and everyday hygiene while respecting the person's comfort, dignity, and privacy.

Meals and feeding assistance

Help prepare or serve meals, assist with eating when required, and support the person's normal meal routine.

Medication reminders

Remind the person to take medications according to the schedule provided by their healthcare professional. Please note: medication reminders are strictly intended to support adherence and are not the same as prescribing medicines or independently changing dosages.

Everyday assistance

Help the person follow their daily routine and provide practical support with ordinary activities around the home.

Companionship

Offer friendly company and support to help the person feel comfortable, listened to, and reassured during daily activities.

Important Distinction: Non-Clinical Assistance

Caregivers provide non-clinical assistance only. They must not be presented as substitutes for registered nurses when a clinical procedure or professional nursing assessment is required. Injections, wound cleaning, dressings, and medical procedures are not performed by caregivers.

Clinical Healthcare Procedures

Registered Nurses — Professional Nursing Care at Home

When a person needs clinical attention but prefers to receive care at home, a registered nurse may help with appropriate nursing procedures and monitoring. The care provided depends on the patient's condition, clinical requirements, and the nurse's qualifications.

All clinical procedures are carried out under professional medical guidelines and treating physician authorization.

A qualified registered nurse performing vital signs assessment and blood pressure check

Clinical Services Provided by Registered Nurses

The following clinical care activities may be provided based on patient assessment and physician authorization:

Injections

Eligible injections (intravenous, intramuscular, or subcutaneous) may be administered by a qualified nurse when appropriately prescribed or authorized by a physician and clinically suitable for home delivery.

Wound care and dressing changes

Nursing professionals provide appropriate post-surgical wound assessments, sterile cleaning, dressing changes, suture care, and bed-sore management according to clinical requirements.

Nursing assessments and monitoring

Regular monitoring of baseline vital signs including blood pressure, pulse rate, oxygen saturation, temperature, and blood glucose, with clear daily logs shared with treating physicians.

Prescribed treatment support

Nurses assist with appropriate prescribed medical treatments within their professional scope, such as IV infusion therapy, nebulization sessions, and oxygen support setup.

Other nursing procedures

Catheter insertion and care, Ryle's tube feeding assistance, tracheostomy routine care, and specialized clinical recovery procedures confirmed on an individual patient evaluation basis.
